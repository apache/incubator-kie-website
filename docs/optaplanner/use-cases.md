OptaPlanner optimizes plans and schedules with _hard constraints_ and _soft
constraints_. It **reduces costs** substantially, improves **service quality**,
**fulfills employee wishes** and **lowers carbon emissions**.

- **Vehicle routing** — quick router for a fleet of vehicles.
- **Employee rostering** — assign shifts to employees by skills and availability.
- **Maintenance scheduling** — timely upkeep of machinery and equipment.
- **Conference scheduling** — schedule speakers and talks by availability and topic.
- **School timetabling** — compact schedules for teachers and students.
- **Task assignment** — assign tasks by priority, skills and affinity.
- **Cloud optimization** — bin packing and defragmentation of cloud resources.
- **Job shop scheduling** — reduce makespan for assembly lines.
