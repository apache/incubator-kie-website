SonataFlow

A platform for building cloud-native workflow applications. Use it for the orchestration and choreography of your services and events.

Orchestration​

Coordinate the services in your architecture into end-to-end workflows, with a workflow in control of every call.

Choreography​

Let workflows react to events and publish their own, so services collaborate without a central controller.

An open standard​

Workflows are written against the CNCF Serverless Workflow specification, a vendor-neutral standard for describing workflows declaratively. SonataFlow worked with version 0.8 of the specification.

Integrations​

Connect workflows to the services and events already in your architecture — see Integrations for the full list.
